## Differences between sources

- His own site's Sobre page says he is influenced by the 1980s and 1990s; his site's May 2024 press piece says the 1990s. The site uses the 1980s and 1990s (his own words and his label's).
- A December 2022 piece on his own site calls Giant Little Victories his first EP; his own site later names Green Sofa Session as the EP (end of 2023) and his label calls Giant Little Victories his first album. The site follows the later sources.

- Postal (2024-06-28) says he "recently released" his debut album; Epopeia's agenda and Apple Music date the album's release to November 2024 (Apple lists To the Bone as first released on 2024-06-13). The site uses the November 2024 date.
- Apple Music lists the album as "℗ 2025 Epopeia Records"; the site uses the release date, not the copyright line.
