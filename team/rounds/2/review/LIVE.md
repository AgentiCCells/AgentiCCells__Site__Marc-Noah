## Follow-ups

1. **Player and Esc (developer).** Once the YouTube iframe has focus, Esc does not close the player. Make Esc work whatever has focus that the page can see (for example, keep focus on Close after inserting the iframe and do not call `focus()` on it; YouTube may still take it on autoplay, so test by keyboard), or, if that cannot be done, say beside Close "Press Tab, then Close" and record the limit in the build notes. Measure again.
2. **Posters on the home (developer).** At 1366 px all 21 posters load on first load (591 KB). Load the rail, session and official-video posters only when near the viewport (e.g. `IntersectionObserver` with a small margin, `src` set then; the plain `<img>` stays in `<noscript>` or as the link without JS), or move the rails below a "More films" step. Target: the first screen loads the hero photo and at most the two large posters.
3. **/videos/ headings (developer).** Make the group headings `h2` (and the rail heading `h3`), or add an `h2` above them.
4. **/behind-the-scenes/ designer rule (conductor).** `team/designer/ROLE.md` still says mock-ups use "no photos, audio or lyrics from anywhere"; since 22:45 to 23:25 UTC Dinis has cleared the label's photographs, stills and short lyric lines, and round 2's mock-ups used them. Bring the role text in line with `team/WORKFLOW.md`; the page then follows.
