# Round 1: critic's notes on the live site

By critic@AgentiCCells.marc-noah, 17 November 2026. Read: the live site v0.1.5 (home, /songs/, /algarve/, /live/, /about/, /listen/, /d/ and its five designs), the r1-before screenshots at 390 and 1366 px, and the two model sites (yiheng-wang and gabriel-dacruz.agenticcells.site), all on 2026-11-17. Facts used here come only from data/facts.mjs. These are proposals for the designer, ranked.

What made the models feel like a person: a photo of them near the top; one plain sentence that says what they do ("He can take a song, write it out for a full orchestra..."); one concrete story told in full rather than a list of everything; sources as small numbered markers, not sentences. Marc's site has none of these yet.

1. **Home, the hero: nothing to press that plays a song.** A listener lands on three claims ("He lives in the Algarve. He is the composer. These are his songs.") and has to scroll past three cards and a map before reaching a song title. Direction: put one song in the hero, with its official link: No More Dreaming or Roadtrip, each the most played song in his label's catalogue for a month in 2025. The big "Listen" button then opens the album, not a scroll.

2. **Home, the order: the Algarve comes fourth, as a small map under the cards.** The drawn map, with Quarteira marked as home, is the strongest picture on the site and the clearest way to say "he lives here". The Coast design describes the map leading; the live home does not do that. Direction: move the map up into or next to the hero, with Quarteira the first thing the eye finds. Until Marc's photos arrive, it is the face of the page; keep a marked slot for a photo so it is easy to drop in.

3. **Home and /about/, card 2 "He is the composer": the claim is in his label's voice and hedged.** "His label calls him a singer and composer..." reads as second-hand. His own first-person words on the album sit two screens lower. Direction: put a short line of his own words beside the claim (for example "My first album... is a message of personal overcoming and of celebration"), and on /songs/ give each track a credit line once Marc confirms who wrote what (open question in NEEDS). Do not write a credit before he does.

4. **Every page, the "Sources: ... ; read 2026-11-17." sentence after every paragraph.** On /live/ it repeats on all 33 rows; on home it follows every card and every date. To a listener or booker it reads as a fact sheet, not a musician. Direction: small numbered markers, as both model sites use, with one sources list per page and the read date stated once.

5. **/songs/, the track list: eight songs as lengths and release dates.** Only Roadtrip says anything about the song, and that is the best story on the site: filmed in Sydney, Salir, Quarteira, Faro and Olhão, with his own words on it. Direction: make Roadtrip the featured song at the top of /songs/, its places drawn as a route on the same map and linked to /algarve/. That one item joins "he lives in the Algarve" and "these are his songs". Give the other tracks the facts we have (single, video, most played) more room, and drop "first released" dates to small type.

6. **/live/ and /listen/, for a programmer: no two-minute answer.** /live/ opens with "No future dates" and then a long past list; the booking route is a paragraph telling you to find a button on another site. Direction: one block at the top of /live/ for programmers: the three formats (solo 60 min, duo 70 min, band 75 min, 6 musicians and 4 crew, indoor and outdoor), five stages he has played (Sons no Castelo in Loulé, Festival Muralhas in Faro, the Fortaleza de Sagres opening, Festival Emersivo, Rock na Ribeira), and one button straight to his Epopeia artist page.

7. **Header and footer, every page: the site talks about itself.** "Five designs" and "Use with AI" sit in the main menu at the same weight as Songs; the footer and the boxed note on /songs/ say what is not copied and that the site came from a factory. A listener reads that as unfinished. Direction: menu of Songs, The Algarve, Live, About, Listen and book; move designs, AI, version and copying notes to one quiet footer line.

8. **Home, /about/ and all five designs: the same three cards, word for word.** By the second page a reader has read them twice; in /d/ each design repeats them, so the designs differ in skin, not in what leads. Direction: the three cards once, on home. /about/ opens with his story and his words; each design leads with its own thing (Songbook with the songs, Tour Poster with the dates and towns, Azulejo with the eight tiles).

9. **Home and /songs/, the album quote: 70 words in Portuguese and 70 in English, in full, twice per page at 390 px.** On a phone it fills more than a screen. Direction: one short line as the pull quote, the full text on /about/. Note that the Algarve programmers and press who book him write in Portuguese, and the site has no Portuguese version (the gabriel-dacruz model has one); worth a decision by Dinis.

10. **/live/, the list itself: small errors a booker notices.** "25 July 2025 · Loulé · Loulé" names the town twice where the venue should be; the jury seat, two radio slots, a video premiere and the album release sit in a list headed "27 concerts". Direction: write "venue not named by his label" where none is given, and show non-concert entries apart from concerts (or drop them from this list).
