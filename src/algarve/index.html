The Algarve

Marc Noah lives in the south of Portugal, in the Algarve. His own site names Quarteira, on the coast between Faro and Albufeira, and most of his concerts are in the towns around it.123

Places

  • Home

    Quarteira

    Where he lives, according to his own site. He played the town’s New Year celebrations on the seafront in December 2022, the Centro Autárquico in November 2024 and the Mercadinho de Verão in July 2025, and filmed part of the Roadtrip video here.24536

    Loulé

    The town he has played most: the Cine Teatro Louletano, Bafo de Baco, the Noite Branca, the castle (Sons no Castelo) and the Festival Emersivo.738

  • Played

    Salir

    A village in the hills north of Loulé. He opened the night of the Festa da Espiga here with his band in May 2024; one of the places in the Roadtrip video.96

    Boliqueime Food Festival, July 2024.3

    Faro

Beyond the Algarve

  • The Azores

    Ponta Delgada (Outro Lado), Ribeira Grande (Vulcana, and the Beach Club Surf & Snack at Santa Bárbara), Rádio Atlântida and a private show, October 2024.3816

  • The rest of mainland Portugal

    Leiria, Viseu, Paços de Ferreira, Vila Nova de Famalicão, Torres Vedras, Almada and Vila Ruiva, November 2024, around the album launch. Earlier: Tokyo ao Vivo in Lisbon (July 2023) and the 7arte Café in Castro Verde (December 2022).37

  • Sydney

    One of the places in the Roadtrip video that, in his label’s words, marked his path.6
