Marc Noah

Singer-songwriter, Algarve

An Australian-Portuguese singer-songwriter living in Quarteira, on the Algarve coast. He writes and composes his own songs, shaped by the sounds of the 1980s and 1990s.

The composer and his songs

On his own site Marc calls himself a cantautor (a singer-songwriter: someone who sings the songs he writes). The songs on this site are his. See the songs for his releases, each with a link to listen.

Where he lives and plays

Marc lives in Quarteira and plays across the Algarve. Regional press has reported concerts in Loulé, Olhão and Sagres. See the Algarve and live.
