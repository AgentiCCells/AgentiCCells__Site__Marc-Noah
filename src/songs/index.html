The songs of Marc Noah

He writes the songs he sings

Marc Noah is a cantautor: he writes the songs he sings. These are his.12 His first album, eight songs, November 2024, on Epopeia Records, a label with an office in Faro.341

Album on Spotify Apple Music YouTube

Featured song · track 6

Roadtrip

His label says the video was filmed in places that marked his path, among them Sydney, Salir, Quarteira, Faro and Olhão.5 In March 2025 it was the most played song in the label’s catalogue.6 His label’s article on it calls him the song’s author.5

    Green Sofa Session

    His first EP, released at the end of 2023: three songs, Lie to Me, Sunshine and Mother, each in a studio version and a live version, and his first physical release. The live versions were filmed as the Green Sofa Sessions, on the green sofa from his living room. Sold with tickets for his March 2025 concert in Loulé.711125
