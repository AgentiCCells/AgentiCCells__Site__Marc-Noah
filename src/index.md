# Marc Noah

Singer-songwriter, Algarve.

An Australian-Portuguese singer-songwriter living in Quarteira, on the Algarve coast. He writes and composes his own songs, shaped by the sounds of the 1980s and 1990s.

Source: the brief's reading of his own page, https://www.marcnoah.pt/sobre (2026-11-17), to be checked again before Marc approves this version.

## The composer and his songs

On his own site Marc calls himself a *cantautor* (a singer-songwriter: someone who sings the songs he writes). Releases reported so far, all to be confirmed against his own pages: the album Giant Little Victories; the EP Green Sofa Session (late 2023); songs Lie to Me, Sunshine, Mother and To The Bone. See https://marc-noah.agenticcells.site/songs/

## Where he lives and plays

Marc lives in Quarteira. Regional press has reported concerts in Loulé (Sons no Castelo), Olhão (República 14) and Sagres (Fortaleza de Sagres programme). See https://marc-noah.agenticcells.site/algarve/ and https://marc-noah.agenticcells.site/live/
