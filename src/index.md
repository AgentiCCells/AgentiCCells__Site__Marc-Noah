# Marc Noah

Singer-songwriter, Algarve. A Portuguese-Australian singer-songwriter who lives in the Algarve and writes the songs he sings.

Every fact below names its public source; each was read on 2026-11-17. Marc approves every change about him. Version __VERSION__.

## His story

### He lives in the Algarve

Marc Noah lives in the south of Portugal, in the Algarve. His own site names Quarteira, on the coast between Faro and Albufeira, and most of his concerts are in the towns around it. (Sources: [Dinis Cruz, 17 Nov 2026](https://github.com/AgentiCCells/AgentiCCells__Site__Factory/blob/dev/requests/2026-11-17__marc-noah/brief.md), [marcnoah.pt (archived 15 Dec 2025)](https://arquivo.pt/noFrame/replay/20251215022621/https://www.marcnoah.pt/sobre), [Epopeia artist page](https://www.epopeia.eu/artistas/marc-noah))

### He is the composer

His label calls him a singer and composer (cantor e compositor) and a cantautor: he writes the songs he sings. His songs, it says, come from real episodes in a darker period of his life, moving from isolation into the light. (Sources: [Epopeia artist page](https://www.epopeia.eu/artistas/marc-noah), [Dinis Cruz, 17 Nov 2026](https://github.com/AgentiCCells/AgentiCCells__Site__Factory/blob/dev/requests/2026-11-17__marc-noah/brief.md))

### These are his songs

Eight of them make up his first album, Giant Little Victories, released in November 2024 on Epopeia Records, a label with an office in Faro. Before it came the EP Green Sofa Session and a run of singles and videos, starting with Lie to Me in February 2023. (Sources: [Epopeia agenda, album release](https://www.epopeia.eu/agenda/marc-noah-giant-little-victories-lp-2024), [Apple Music](https://music.apple.com/pt/artist/marc-noah/1846838057), [Epopeia artist page](https://www.epopeia.eu/artistas/marc-noah))

## In his own words

On Giant Little Victories:

> O meu 1º álbum: "Giant Little Victories" é uma mensagem de superação pessoal e de celebração. É o querer reforçar a necessidade de celebrar quem somos, quem amamos, o nosso crescimento e as pequenas vitórias da vida, é a reafirmação de que quando nos encontramos no escuro e a luz passa a ser a constante passamos a ter o poder da decisão novamente.

My first album, "Giant Little Victories", is a message of personal overcoming and of celebration. It is wanting to reinforce the need to celebrate who we are, who we love, our growth and the small victories of life; it is the reaffirmation that when we find ourselves in the dark and the light becomes the constant, we have the power of decision again. (our translation) (Source: [Epopeia artist page](https://www.epopeia.eu/artistas/marc-noah))

On Roadtrip, which he calls a turning point:

> É um encarar a vida de uma forma mais leve e positiva. É a ansia pela liberdade das amarras da vida rotineira do dia-a-dia.

It is facing life in a lighter, more positive way. It is the longing to be free of the ties of everyday routine. (our translation) (Source: [Epopeia Jornal, 28 Feb 2025](https://www.epopeia.eu/jornal/marc-noah-embarca-numa-viagem-musical-com-concerto-unico-em-loulo))

On To The Bone, his fifth single:

> O To The Bone foi escrito numa fase da minha vida em que os pensamentos estavam constantemente a ocupar demasiado espaço na mente. Percebi que tinha que aceita-los e depois deixa-los ir para que o espaço que estavam a ocupar, pudesse dar lugar a coisas boas.

To The Bone was written at a time in my life when thoughts were constantly taking up too much space in my mind. I realised I had to accept them and then let them go, so that the space they were taking up could make room for good things. (our translation) (Source: [marcnoah.pt press, 27 Aug 2024](https://arquivo.pt/noFrame/replay/20250311190951/https://www.marcnoah.pt/press/marc-noah-lanca-novo-single))

On the Green Sofa Sessions:

> Um sofá verde que vive num canto da minha sala ganhou nova vida nas GREEN SOFA SESSIONS.

A green sofa that lives in a corner of my living room found new life in the GREEN SOFA SESSIONS. (our translation) (Source: [marcnoah.pt, Green Sofa Session (archived 15 Dec 2025)](https://arquivo.pt/noFrame/replay/20251215022918/https://www.marcnoah.pt/singles/green-sofa-session))

## His story, as his label tells it

> O cantautor luso-australiano partilha a sua alma em canções que nos envolvem.

The Portuguese-Australian singer-songwriter shares his soul in songs that wrap around us.

> Marc Noah, cantor e compositor luso-australiano, foi salvo pela música durante toda a sua vida. Ao lidar com questões da adolescência, descobriu que a música era o único sitio onde se sentia são e salvo.

Marc Noah, Portuguese-Australian singer and composer, has been saved by music all his life. Dealing with the troubles of adolescence, he found that music was the only place where he felt safe and sound.

> Depois de trabalhar no “The Boomerang Bar”, o seu caminho levou-o naturalmente ao que ama e quando o Covid-19 chegou ao mundo, encontrou tempo para organizar as suas letras e melodias.

After working at “The Boomerang Bar”, his path led him naturally to what he loves, and when Covid-19 reached the world he found time to put his lyrics and melodies in order.

> Influenciado pela música dos anos 80 e 90, [...] do pop ao grunge, dos musicais ao rock, tocou em diversas bandas de covers, sempre evitando ter um “verdadeiro trabalho diurno” que nunca parecia adequar-se às suas medidas. Uma mistura suave de Pop/Indie/Rock é o que melhor define o seu som quando se destaca sozinho ou com sua banda.

Influenced by the music of the 1980s and 1990s, [...] from pop to grunge, from musicals to rock, he played in several cover bands, always avoiding a “real day job” that never seemed to fit him. A gentle mix of pop, indie and rock best describes his sound, whether he stands out alone or with his band.

> Com o EP “Green Sofa Session” e o álbum “Giant Little Victories” lançados, as suas músicas têm origem em episódios da vida real de um período mais sombrio de sua vida, passando do isolamento para a luz, enquanto mostra a sua música em qualquer palco onde tem a oportunidade de tocar.

With the EP “Green Sofa Session” and the album “Giant Little Victories” out, his songs come from real-life episodes of a darker period of his life, moving from isolation to the light, while he shows his music on any stage where he gets the chance to play.

The [...] marks a list of artists who influenced him, left out here; read it on the label page. (Source: [Epopeia artist page](https://www.epopeia.eu/artistas/marc-noah))
