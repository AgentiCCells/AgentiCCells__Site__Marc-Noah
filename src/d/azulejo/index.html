Marc Noah

SpotifyApple MusicYouTube

He lives in the Algarve

Marc Noah lives in the south of Portugal, in the Algarve. His own site names Quarteira, on the coast between Faro and Albufeira, and most of his concerts are in the towns around it.

Sources: Dinis Cruz, 17 Nov 2026, marcnoah.pt, via the factory brief, Epopeia artist page; read 2026-11-17.

He is the composer

His label calls him a singer and composer (cantor e compositor) and a cantautor: he writes the songs he sings. His songs, it says, come from real episodes in a darker period of his life, moving from isolation into the light.

Sources: Epopeia artist page, Dinis Cruz, 17 Nov 2026; read 2026-11-17.

These are his songs

Eight of them make up his first album, Giant Little Victories, released in November 2024 on Epopeia Records, a label with an office in Faro. Before it came the EP Green Sofa Session and a run of singles and videos, starting with Lie to Me in February 2023.

Sources: Epopeia agenda, album release, Apple Music, Epopeia artist page; read 2026-11-17.

His Algarve

  • Quarteira (home): Where he lives, according to his own site. He played the Centro Autárquico in November 2024 and the Mercadinho de Verão in July 2025, and filmed part of the Roadtrip video here. Sources: marcnoah.pt, via the factory brief, Epopeia artist page, Epopeia Jornal, 28 Feb 2025; read 2026-11-17.
  • Loulé: The town he has played most: Bafo de Baco, the Noite Branca, the castle (Sons no Castelo) and the Festival Emersivo. Sources: Epopeia artist page, A Voz do Algarve, 5 Aug 2026; read 2026-11-17.
  • Salir: A village in the hills north of Loulé; one of the places in the Roadtrip video. Source: Epopeia Jornal, 28 Feb 2025; read 2026-11-17.
  • Boliqueime: Boliqueime Food Festival, July 2024. Source: Epopeia artist page; read 2026-11-17.
  • Faro: His label has an office here. Festival Muralhas (2025), FNAC Faro (2024), and a place in the Roadtrip video. He sat on the jury of the XXIV Fartuna at the Teatro das Figuras in 2025. Sources: Epopeia artist page, Epopeia Jornal, 28 Feb 2025; read 2026-11-17.
  • Olhão: Two sessions at República 14 in March 2023; a place in the Roadtrip video. Sources: Mais Algarve, 22 Mar 2023, Epopeia Jornal, 28 Feb 2025; read 2026-11-17.
  • Pechão: Rock na Ribeira, Parque da Família, June 2025. Source: Epopeia Jornal, 3 Jun 2025; read 2026-11-17.
  • Albufeira (Guia): FNAC Guia, November 2024. Source: Epopeia artist page; read 2026-11-17.
  • Portimão: Food Trucks Festival, August 2026. Source: Epopeia artist page; read 2026-11-17.
  • Raposeira: Junta de Freguesia, November 2024. Source: Epopeia artist page; read 2026-11-17.
  • Sagres: His showcase opened the Fortaleza Cultural programme at the Fortaleza de Sagres on 5 July 2024. Source: Postal do Algarve, 28 Jun 2024; read 2026-11-17.

The Azores: Ponta Delgada (Outro Lado), Ribeira Grande (Vulcana, and the Beach Club Surf & Snack at Santa Bárbara), Rádio Atlântida and a private show, October 2024. The rest of mainland Portugal: Leiria, Viseu, Paços de Ferreira, Vila Nova de Famalicão, Torres Vedras, Almada and Vila Ruiva, November 2024, around the album launch. Sydney: One of the places in the Roadtrip video that, in his label’s words, marked his path. Sources: Epopeia artist page, Epopeia Jornal, 28 Feb 2025; read 2026-11-17.
