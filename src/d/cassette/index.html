Liner notes

He lives in the Algarve

Marc Noah lives in the south of Portugal, in the Algarve. His own site names Quarteira, on the coast between Faro and Albufeira, and most of his concerts are in the towns around it.

Sources: Dinis Cruz, 17 Nov 2026, marcnoah.pt, via the factory brief, Epopeia artist page; read 2026-11-17.

He is the composer

His label calls him a singer and composer (cantor e compositor) and a cantautor: he writes the songs he sings. His songs, it says, come from real episodes in a darker period of his life, moving from isolation into the light.

Sources: Epopeia artist page, Dinis Cruz, 17 Nov 2026; read 2026-11-17.

These are his songs

Eight of them make up his first album, Giant Little Victories, released in November 2024 on Epopeia Records, a label from Faro. Before it came the EP Green Sofa Session and a run of singles and videos, starting with Lie to Me in February 2023.

Sources: Epopeia agenda, album release, Apple Music, Epopeia artist page; read 2026-11-17.

Recorded and played in

  • Quarteira (home): Where he lives, according to his own site. He played the Centro Autárquico in November 2024 and the Mercadinho de Verão in July 2025, and filmed part of the Roadtrip video here.
  • Loulé: The town he has played most: Bafo de Baco, the Noite Branca, the castle (Sons no Castelo) and the Festival Emersivo.
  • Salir: A village in the hills north of Loulé; one of the places in the Roadtrip video.
  • Boliqueime: Boliqueime Food Festival, July 2024.
  • Faro: Home of his label. Festival Muralhas (2025), FNAC Faro (2024), and a place in the Roadtrip video. He sat on the jury of the XXIV Fartuna at the Teatro das Figuras in 2025.
  • Olhão: Two sessions at República 14 in March 2023; a place in the Roadtrip video.
  • Pechão: Rock na Ribeira, Parque da Família, June 2025.
  • Albufeira (Guia): FNAC Guia, November 2024.
  • Portimão: Food Trucks Festival, August 2026.
  • Raposeira: Junta de Freguesia, November 2024.
  • Sagres: His showcase opened the Fortaleza Cultural programme at the Fortaleza de Sagres on 5 July 2024.
  • The Azores: Ponta Delgada (Outro Lado), Ribeira Grande (Vulcana), Rádio Atlântida and a private show, October 2024.
  • The rest of mainland Portugal: Leiria, Viseu, Paços de Ferreira, Vila Nova de Famalicão, Torres Vedras, Almada and Vila Ruiva, November 2024, around the album launch.
  • Sydney: One of the places in the Roadtrip video that, in his label’s words, marked his path.
