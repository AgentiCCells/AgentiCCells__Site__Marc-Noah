About Marc

Marc Noah is an Australian-Portuguese singer-songwriter. He lives in Quarteira, in the Algarve, and writes and composes the songs he sings.

In his own framing

On his own site, in Portuguese, Marc describes himself as:

  • luso-australiano: Portuguese-Australian;
  • cantautor: a singer-songwriter, someone who sings his own songs;
  • living in Quarteira;
  • influenced by the sounds of the 1980s and 1990s.
