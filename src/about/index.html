About Marc

A Portuguese-Australian singer-songwriter who lives in the Algarve and writes the songs he sings. Sources: Epopeia artist page, Dinis Cruz, 17 Nov 2026; read 2026-11-17.

He lives in the Algarve

Marc Noah lives in the south of Portugal, in the Algarve. His own site names Quarteira, on the coast between Faro and Albufeira, and most of his concerts are in the towns around it. Sources: Dinis Cruz, 17 Nov 2026, marcnoah.pt, via the factory brief, Epopeia artist page; read 2026-11-17.

He is the composer

His label calls him a singer and composer (cantor e compositor) and a cantautor: he writes the songs he sings. His songs, it says, come from real episodes in a darker period of his life, moving from isolation into the light. Sources: Epopeia artist page, Dinis Cruz, 17 Nov 2026; read 2026-11-17.

These are his songs

Eight of them make up his first album, Giant Little Victories, released in November 2024 on Epopeia Records, a label with an office in Faro. Before it came the EP Green Sofa Session and a run of singles and videos, starting with Lie to Me in February 2023. Sources: Epopeia agenda, album release, Apple Music, Epopeia artist page; read 2026-11-17.

His sound and his voice

His label puts it as a gentle mix of pop, indie and rock. The Algarve press writes of an intimate sound and a husky voice (voz rouca) that has become one of his marks. Sources: Epopeia artist page, A Voz do Algarve, 5 Aug 2026; read 2026-11-17.
