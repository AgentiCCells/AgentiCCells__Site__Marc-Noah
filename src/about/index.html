He lives in the Algarve

Marc Noah lives in the south of Portugal, in the Algarve. His own site names Quarteira, on the coast between Faro and Albufeira, and most of his concerts are in the towns around it.231

He is the composer

His label calls him a singer and composer (cantor e compositor) and a cantautor: he writes the songs he sings. His songs, it says, come from real episodes in a darker period of his life, moving from isolation into the light.12

Eight of them make up his first album, Giant Little Victories, released in November 2024 on Epopeia Records, a label with an office in Faro. Before it came the EP Green Sofa Session and a run of singles and videos, starting with Lie to Me in February 2023.451

About Marc Noah

Marc Noah is a Portuguese-Australian singer-songwriter who lives in Quarteira, on the Algarve coast in the south of Portugal. He writes the songs he sings: his own site calls him a "músico cantautor" (a singer-songwriter musician), and his label calls him a singer and composer.312

He is, in his own site’s words, "fortemente influenciado pelas sonoridades dos anos 80 e 90" (strongly influenced by the sounds of the 1980s and 1990s), and his label describes his sound as a gentle mix of pop, indie and rock.31

His EP Green Sofa Session came out at the end of 2023, and his first album, Giant Little Victories, followed in November 2024 on Epopeia Records, a label with an office in Faro. He plays solo, as a duo and with a six-piece band, mostly in the Algarve.3451

His story, as his label tells it

From his artist page at Epopeia Records, in Portuguese as published, with our translation beside each paragraph.1

O cantautor luso-australiano partilha a sua alma em canções que nos envolvem.

The Portuguese-Australian singer-songwriter shares his soul in songs that wrap around us.

Marc Noah, cantor e compositor luso-australiano, foi salvo pela música durante toda a sua vida. Ao lidar com questões da adolescência, descobriu que a música era o único sitio onde se sentia são e salvo.

Marc Noah, Portuguese-Australian singer and composer, has been saved by music all his life. Dealing with the troubles of adolescence, he found that music was the only place where he felt safe and sound.

Depois de trabalhar no “The Boomerang Bar”, o seu caminho levou-o naturalmente ao que ama e quando o Covid-19 chegou ao mundo, encontrou tempo para organizar as suas letras e melodias.

After working at “The Boomerang Bar”, his path led him naturally to what he loves, and when Covid-19 reached the world he found time to put his lyrics and melodies in order.

Influenciado pela música dos anos 80 e 90, [...] do pop ao grunge, dos musicais ao rock, tocou em diversas bandas de covers, sempre evitando ter um “verdadeiro trabalho diurno” que nunca parecia adequar-se às suas medidas. Uma mistura suave de Pop/Indie/Rock é o que melhor define o seu som quando se destaca sozinho ou com sua banda.

Influenced by the music of the 1980s and 1990s, [...] from pop to grunge, from musicals to rock, he played in several cover bands, always avoiding a “real day job” that never seemed to fit him. A gentle mix of pop, indie and rock best describes his sound, whether he stands out alone or with his band.

Com o EP “Green Sofa Session” e o álbum “Giant Little Victories” lançados, as suas músicas têm origem em episódios da vida real de um período mais sombrio de sua vida, passando do isolamento para a luz, enquanto mostra a sua música em qualquer palco onde tem a oportunidade de tocar.

With the EP “Green Sofa Session” and the album “Giant Little Victories” out, his songs come from real-life episodes of a darker period of his life, moving from isolation to the light, while he shows his music on any stage where he gets the chance to play.
